How to Play

Desktop: ←/→ or A/D to move · Space/↑/W to jump (hold for higher) · ↓/S on a hidden pipe.
Mobile: on-screen buttons (◀ ▶ left, ▲ ▼ right).

🪙 Collect coins (+10) · 🦘 Stomp enemies (+100) · 🚩 Touch flags for checkpoints · ⚠️ Avoid spikes and pits · 🕳️ Find the hidden pipe in each level for a bonus room · 👹 Stomp the boss until it falls, then reach the goal flag.

3 lives. Finish all 5 levels to win. Your score is verified by our server before it hits the leaderboard.
